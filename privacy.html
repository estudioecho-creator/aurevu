Information Aurevu processes

Aurevu uses Firebase Authentication for Google sign-in or email and password accounts. Firebase may process your email address, display name, account identifier, authentication information, IP address and security diagnostics. RF SOFTWARE never receives your Google or email password.

When you search for a business, the search text is sent through RF SOFTWARE’s protected server to Google Places. After selection, Aurevu stores the Google Place ID, your display mode and account settings. Business names, addresses, official Google review links and review content are requested from Google Maps when needed and are not stored by RF SOFTWARE as a permanent review database.

In rolling-reviews mode, Google may return up to five reviews, author attribution and source links. Aurevu displays this content only for the kiosk session and does not use review text for analytics or advertising.

Subscription data

Google Play processes payments and payment methods. Aurevu sends the purchase token to its protected server to verify subscription status with Google Play. The server stores the resulting entitlement, expiry time and a one-way hash of the token. RF SOFTWARE does not receive card details.

Use, sharing and retention

Information is used to authenticate your account, find the business you select, generate its official review QR display, verify your subscription, secure the service and provide support. Service providers are Google Firebase, Google Cloud, Google Maps Platform and Google Play. Data may be processed where those providers operate. Aurevu contains no advertising and does not sell personal information.

Account settings remain while your account exists. Operational rate-limit records expire automatically. Google controls data it processes under its own policies. Network traffic is encrypted in transit.

Your choices and deletion

You can change the active business, display mode and language in the app. Use Settings → Delete account and data to delete your Aurevu account, stored preferences and entitlement mirror. You may also request deletion at this page or by email. Subscription cancellation is managed separately in Google Play and deleting an Aurevu account does not itself refund a payment.

Audience and changes

Aurevu is a business utility and is not directed to children. This policy may be updated when the service changes. Google privacy information: policies.google.com/privacy.
