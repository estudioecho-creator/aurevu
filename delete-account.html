Delete your Aurevu account

Aurevu users can delete their account directly in the app:

  1. Open Aurevu and sign in.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Select Delete account and data.
  4. Authenticate again if requested, then confirm deletion.

This deletes the Firebase Authentication account and Aurevu’s stored account settings and subscription mirror. Operational records with no direct profile content expire automatically. Google Play purchase and accounting records are controlled by Google and may be retained for legal and financial obligations.

Deleting the Aurevu account does not cancel an active Google Play subscription. Cancel it first from Google Play’s subscription settings.

If you cannot access the app, email estudioecho@gmail.com from the account email and request “Aurevu account deletion.” We may ask you to verify control of the account before deletion.
